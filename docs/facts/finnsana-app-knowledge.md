## Quick facts
- Name: FinnSana: Learn Finnish. Package app.finnsana. Android only. Version 1.0.0, in closed testing on Google Play (not public yet).

- Tabs: Home, Learn, Practice, Grammar, Me.

- After install Google Play downloads sounds and pictures once (about 700 MB); the first lessons work immediately.
- Three colour themes: Forest (green, default), Terracotta, Midnight (dark).
- NOT in the app (do not mention): YKI test practice, speech recording, human teacher, iOS version, subscriptions.

# FinnSana — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

FinnSana is an app for learning Finnish. This policy explains what happens to
your information when you use it. The short version: **you can use FinnSana as a guest without any account, and
then your learning progress never leaves your device.** Signing in with Google
or with an email address and password is optional: if you do, your progress is
backed up so you can get it back on a new phone (section 2). The app has no
purchases, no advertising and no analytics.

## 0. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is **Kuvadoo**, a sole trader business in Finland:

- Owner (sole trader): **[TO FILL: owner's full legal name]**
- Business ID (Y-tunnus): **[TO FILL: Y-tunnus]**
- Postal address: **[TO FILL: street, postcode, town], Finland**
- Email: **kuvadoo@gmail.com**

## 1. What stays on your device

To remember your learning, FinnSana saves the following **only on your own
device**, inside the app's private storage:

- your lesson and exercise progress, review schedule, mistakes and skill scores,
- your study streak and study time,
- your settings (daily goal, level, theme, sounds) and game best scores,
- the display name and avatar you choose in Me,
- the words you add yourself, with your notes and the pictures you choose for them,
- if you sign in: your account name, email address, profile-picture link,
  account ID and the sign-in session (never your password).

As a guest, this information is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling
the app (or clearing its storage in your phone's settings) deletes all of it.

**Reset progress** (Me → Reset progress) deletes from your phone your review
schedule, lesson history, mistakes, skill scores, study time, streak, best
scores and settings, including the display name and avatar.
**The words you added yourself, with their notes and pictures, are kept**, so
you don't lose what you typed; their reviews start again from the beginning.
To delete them, remove them in **My words**. If you are signed in, the reset is also copied to
your cloud backup and to your other signed-in phones: the progress made before
the reset is removed there too.

## 2. Optional account (Google or email) and cloud backup

You can choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with email** on the first
screen or later in **Me → Account & sync**. Only if you do:

- **With Google:** we receive your Google account's name, email address,
  profile picture and a unique account ID. We never see your Google password.
- **With email:** you give your email address, a password and optionally a
  name. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never
  visible to us. Firebase sends the emails to confirm your address and to reset
  a forgotten password.
- **What is backed up:** your learning progress from section 1 (lesson and
  review progress, streak, study time, settings and best scores) and the
  words you added yourself with their notes. **Not** backed up: the pictures of
  your own words and your display name and avatar (they stay on your phone only).
- **Why:** only to back up your progress and restore it when you sign in on
  another phone or after reinstalling the app. It is not used for advertising,
  profiling or anything else, and it is not sold or shared.
- **Where:** in **Google Firebase**, run by Google on our behalf as our data
  processor. Your progress backup (Cloud Firestore) is stored in the EU
  (Finland, europe-north1). Your sign-in details (Firebase Authentication:
  name, email, picture, account ID) are processed by Google, which may store
  them outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's
  data protection terms (EU Standard Contractual Clauses). When you sign in and
  when the app backs up, Google also logs your IP address and technical details
  of your device and app version for security, and keeps these logs for a
  limited time. FinnSana is used around the world, so if you live outside the
  EU your data is transferred to the EU and to Google's servers as described
  here. Data is encrypted in transit. Security rules let each account read and
  write only its own backup. Google's privacy policy applies:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy and
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Legal basis (EU):** providing the backup service you asked for when you
  created the account (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). You can stop it at any time by
  signing out or deleting your account.

**Signing out** stops the backup on that phone; your progress stays on the
phone and the backup stays in the cloud. **Deleting your account** (Me →
your profile → Delete account) immediately deletes your FinnSana account and
its cloud backup; the progress on your phone stays and you continue as a
guest. So that a deleted backup cannot be re-created (for example by another
phone still signed in to the same account), we keep a record that holds only
the random ID of the deleted account (no name, email or progress). You can
also ask us to delete your account by email (section 8).

## 3. What the app does not do

- No account is required; sign-in is optional (section 2). No phone number is asked for.
- No advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking across other apps.
- No location, contacts, camera or microphone access. FinnSana does not record
  your voice.
- No access to your photo gallery. If you choose to add a picture to one of
  your own words, you pick it with your phone's photo picker: FinnSana
  receives only that one picture, keeps a copy inside the app on your phone
  and never uploads it. Removing the word in My words removes FinnSana's copy
  of the picture; the photo picker may leave a temporary copy in the app's
  cache until your phone clears it, and uninstalling the app removes all of them.
- We do not sell your data.

The voices (computer-generated) and pictures are not sent by us: after you
install the app, **Google Play** downloads them to your phone as part of the
app (about 700 MB, once). After that FinnSana works offline. An internet
connection is used only for that download and for the optional sign-in and
cloud backup.

## 4. How long data is kept

Your learning data stays on your device until you delete it (section 1). If
you signed in, your cloud backup and account are kept for as long as the
account exists, and deleted when you delete the account or ask us to delete
it. Requests by email are handled within 30 days.

## 5. Your rights

If you live in the EU or a similar region, you have the right to:

- see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it,
- have it corrected or deleted,
- receive it in a portable, machine-readable form,
- restrict or object to how it is processed,
- stop the backup at any time (sign out or delete the account), and
- complain to a data protection authority: in Finland the **Data Protection
  Ombudsman** (https://tietosuoja.fi/en), or the authority where you live.

As a guest, all your data is on your own phone and we hold nothing about you.
If you signed in (with Google or email), write to us from that address and we
export or delete your backup; you can also delete it yourself in the app
(Me → your profile → Delete account).

## 6. Children

FinnSana is meant for learners aged 13 and over and is not directed at
younger children. As a guest it collects no personal data. Creating an
account is optional.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address
with a new date.

## 8. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: **kuvadoo@gmail.com** (Kuvadoo, see

## Delete your FinnSana account

FinnSana lets you sign in with Google or with an email address and password to back up your learning progress. You can delete that account and all of its cloud data at any time.

### In the app (fastest)

1. Open **FinnSana**.
2. Go to the **Me** tab and tap your profile at the top.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm. If you signed in with email, enter your password to confirm.

Your FinnSana account and its cloud backup are deleted at once. The progress saved on your phone stays, and you can keep using FinnSana as a guest.

Another phone that is still signed in to the deleted account cannot upload its progress to it again.

### Without the app

Email **kuvadoo@gmail.com** from the address you signed in with, with the subject **"Delete my FinnSana account"**. We delete the account and its backup within 30 days and reply when it is done.

### What is deleted

- Your FinnSana account (your name, email address, profile picture and account ID as stored by FinnSana).
- Your cloud backup: lesson and review progress, streak, study time, settings, best scores and the words you added yourself.

### What is kept

- Nothing in your cloud backup is kept. So that the deleted backup cannot be re-created, a record holding only the random ID of the deleted account (no name, email or progress) is kept. Google keeps its security logs of sign-ins (IP address, device and app version) for a limited time, as described in the privacy policy.
- Progress on your own phone is not touched. **Me → Reset progress** clears your learning progress, settings and best scores but keeps the words you added yourself (delete those in **My words**).
- Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone.

### Who handles your request

Kuvadoo (sole trader), Finland, kuvadoo@gmail.com. Business ID and postal address: see the privacy policy, section 0.
