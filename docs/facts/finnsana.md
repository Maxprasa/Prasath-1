## Features
1. **Nearly 5,000 words with sound and pictures**
   - everyday words from A1 to B2, by topic
   - a picture for almost every word (AI-generated illustrations)
   - words and example sentences spoken by a clear computer-generated Finnish voice, normal or slow
   - a memory trick for every word
   - add your own words
2. **Grammar that finally makes sense**
   - over 100 grammar lessons (cases, verb types, partitive, past tense, conditional, passive and more)
   - an overview first, then one small rule at a time
   - spoken lesson explanations (computer-generated voice)
   - spoken Finnish (puhekieli) explained
3. **Practice that feels like a game**
   - Word Match, Speed Round, True or False, Fix the Mistake, Spell what you hear, flashcards,
     verb trainer, sentence builder, listening and reading practice
4. **Smart review**
   - spaced repetition brings each word back just before you forget it
   - targeted practice for the patterns behind your mistakes
5. **Made for real learners**
   - CEFR levels A1–B2
   - everyday situations
   - works offline once the sounds and pictures have downloaded (about 700 MB, once, Wi-Fi is best)
   - three colour themes
   - optional sign-in with Google or email to back up progress

## Account deletion
Owner-confirmed 2026-10-07: the in-app path is **Me tab → tap your profile (at the top) → Delete account**
(the brief's "Me → Account → Delete account" was outdated).

You can use FinnSana without an account. If you signed in: open the app → Me tab → tap your profile → Delete account.
This deletes your sign-in account and your cloud backup (progress, settings). Your progress on the phone is
removed with "Reset progress" or by uninstalling the app. If you cannot open the app, write to
kuvadoo@gmail.com from the email address of your account and we delete it within 30 days.
