# Business names in Finland: aputoiminimi, marketing name, rename, trademark (2026-10-07)

Question: Kuvadoo is a sole trader (yksityinen elinkeinonharjoittaja). He wants to use the older local photography
brand "Hämeen Films" (formerly "Hämee Films"). Does he have to register it, and how?
This is research, not legal advice. **An accountant or PRH (Trade Register advice line +358 29 509 5030) should confirm
before anything is filed.** This file lives in `docs/` and is not uploaded. Nothing here changes the apps site:
the footer stays `© 2026 Kuvadoo, Finland · kuvadoo@gmail.com` (CLAUDE.md rule 4) and no photography brand belongs
on apps.kuvadoo.fi.

## Recommendations

1. **No re-registration of the business is needed.** A sole trader can add a registered auxiliary trade name
   (aputoiminimi) to the existing Kuvadoo registration with a notification of changes at ytj.fi: **75 EUR per
   name** (2026). Y-tunnus does not change.
2. The auxiliary name **must cover only part of the business.** Check the registered line of business first. If
   only photography is registered, "Hämeen Films" for all photography would be the whole business and is not allowed.
   Either register the apps/software line too (55 EUR line-of-business change), or tie the auxiliary name to a narrower
   part (e.g. photo and video services in the Häme region). PRH/accountant to confirm the wording.
3. **If he only uses "Hämeen Films" as a marketing name, invoices and contracts must still show "Kuvadoo" (the
   registered name) and the Y-tunnus.** Vero.fi says the seller name on an invoice is the toiminimi or aputoiminimi
   entered in the Trade Register.
4. "Hämeen Films" (place name plus descriptive word) is the kind of name PRH says is acceptable *in combination*, but
   it will give **weak protection**. Do not count on stopping others using "Häme" or "Films".
5. **Trademark:** not worth it for the local photography brand. For an international app brand, an EU trademark
   (850 EUR, one class) is the relevant tool. A Finnish trade name gives no rights outside Finland. Decide only once
   the app brand is final.
6. Run the free PRH name check (nimipalvelu) and ytj.fi search yourself before filing. I could not check sole-trader
   names (see section 5).

## 1. Auxiliary trade name (aputoiminimi)

Observations:
- Traders in the Trade Register "can register an auxiliary company name for running a certain part of their
  operations". A private trader decides this alone. "The business run under the auxiliary company name must not cover
  the company's whole line of business". If there is only one line of business, it "must be limited, for example,
  geographically". You must state which line of business it applies to. It must not contain a company-form suffix.
  https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet/aputoiminimi.html and
  https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet.html
- A registered auxiliary name cannot be edited. It can be removed (handling fee 55 EUR) and a new one entered. Same
  sources.
- **How:** use a notification of changes at ytj.fi (Finnish/Swedish only), or paper form Y6. Private traders are
  exempt from the 2026 mandatory online filing, but paper takes longer and the fee must be paid in advance.
  On Y6: "Describe which of the company's activities are conducted under each auxiliary company name."
  https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/muutokset_rekisteritietoihin/private_traders.html ·
  https://www.ytj.fi/en/index/notifications/instructions-for-filling-in-notification-on-amendments-or-termination-of-business/instructions-for-filling-in-notification-on-amendments-or-termination-of-business-y6.html ·
  https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/trade-register-mandatory-online-filing/frequently-asked-questions.html (search snippet; not opened)
- **Fee 2026:** "notifying a company name or parallel company name, or an auxiliary name": **75 EUR per company
  name**. Private trader change of line of business: 55 EUR. 0% VAT.
  https://www.prh.fi/en/presentation_and_duties/uutislistaus/announcements/2026/companies-organisations-auditors-handling-fees-changes-1.1.2026.html
  (The fee decree is Maksuasetus 1216/2025, valid 1.1–31.12.2026:
  https://www.prh.fi/material/sites/prh/attachments/tietoaprhsta/maksuasetusl-llehikoinen/9mh0gc98g/Maksuasetus_1216_2025.pdf)
- **Processing time:** on the PRH status page, updated 5.10.2026, "Other notifications of changes filed in the online
  service at ytj.fi" were being processed from arrival date 15.9.2026. That is about a 3-week queue. "Other notifications
  ... on online or paper form" were at 4.9.2026, about 4.5 weeks. https://www.prh.fi/en/processing-times/kasittelyajat.html
  In March 2026 PRH said processing was "longer than usual":
  https://www.prh.fi/en/presentation_and_duties/uutislistaus/announcements/2026/trade-register-processing-times-13.3.2026.html
- **Protection:** registration gives an exclusive right. Other businesses in Finland cannot use a confusingly similar
  name. Confusability "is usually assessed within the same or very similar lines of business". A name can also be
  protected by becoming established through use. https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet.html ·
  https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet/nimiohjeet.html.
  Trade Names Act 128/1979 §2–3: registration gives an exclusive right in Finland, and an established (unregistered)
  name is protected only where it is established (WIPO copy of an older consolidated text, amendments up to 1994):
  https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/463489

## 2. Marketing name only (no registration)

Observations:
- I found **no PRH page that defines "markkinointinimi"** or forbids using an unregistered name in marketing. PRH
  "recommends using the registered company name in the form entered in the Trade Register". It also does not recommend
  publishing a name before it is registered. https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet.html
- No protection unless the name becomes "established" (generally known among the target customers): Trade Names Act
  §2, https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/463489. Suomen Yrittäjät: "Rekisteröimätön nimi voidaan riitauttaa."
  https://www.yrittajat.fi/tietopankki/sopimukset-ja-oikeudet/immateriaalioikeudet/yrityksen-toiminimi/ (search
  snippet).
- **Invoices:** the seller name should be the "toiminimi tai aputoiminimi, jolla elinkeinonharjoittaja on merkitty
  kaupparekisteriin". Also: "Elinkeinonharjoittajan on merkittävä Y-tunnuksensa elinkeinotoimintaan liittyviin
  asiakirjoihin." https://vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/ohje-hakusivu/48090/laskutusvaatimukset-arvonlis%C3%A4verotuksessa/
  (the page I opened is the 2019–2023 version; a newer version is linked there, so check it).
- **Consumer distance sales:** the consumer must be told the trader's name, geographic address, email and phone
  before the contract (Minilex summary, https://www.minilex.fi/a/et%C3%A4myynti-ja-peruminen, search snippet). I did
  not reach the KKV page or the current statute text. **Not verified.** An accountant should confirm what kuvadoo.fi
  must show. The current site shows no Y-tunnus (fetched https://www.kuvadoo.fi/, footer "© 2026 KUVADOO.fi").

## 3. Renaming the main trade name instead

- Private trader "Change of company name: 75 euros". Filed online at ytj.fi with a notification of changes.
  https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/muutokset_rekisteritietoihin/private_traders.html
- **Y-tunnus:** the Y6 instructions say a new Business ID is needed only when the *legal form* changes. No page I
  read says a name change creates a new ID, and none says explicitly that it keeps the old one. (Y6 link above.)
  PRH to confirm. A new name has no protection until it is registered.
- Renaming Kuvadoo would also affect apps.kuvadoo.fi, the publisher line and the app-store developer identity. It is
  not needed for this goal.

## 4. Trademark vs trade name

- A trade name identifies the business. A trademark identifies goods/services and protects their use in sales and
  marketing.
- **Finland (PRH), 2026:** 250 EUR online for one class, +100 EUR per extra class, paper 300 EUR. Renewal 250 EUR.
  https://prh.fi/en/price-lists/trademark_fees/fees_for_trademark_applications.html. Valid 10 years:
  https://prh.fi/en/trademarks/maintain_and_renew_your_trademark/renew_your_trademark_every_10_years.html
- **EU (EUIPO):** 850 EUR online for one class, 50 EUR for the 2nd, 150 EUR for each from the 3rd.
  https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/trade-marks/before-applying/fees-payments (search snippet; the page returned 403 to me).
- The 2026 EUIPO SME Fund paid back 75% of trade mark fees, up to 700 EUR. A search snippet says the trade mark voucher
  is now closed because the funds ran out. https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/sme-corner/sme-fund/2026 (403 to me. Not verified.)
- Assessment (mine): (a) For a local photography brand, an aputoiminimi (75 EUR) gives Finland-wide name protection
  for that line of business. A place-name brand would be a weak trademark anyway. (b) For an international app brand,
  a Finnish trade name has no effect abroad. An EU trademark in the software class is the relevant option once the
  app name is final (see also `docs/research/2026-10-app-brand-domain.md`).

## 6. Place names (Häme / Hämeen)

- PRH: not acceptable *on their own*: "General place-names (Helsinki Ltd)", a simple indication of the type of
  business, and names of goods or services. These can be registered when combined with something else, e.g. "a
  place name, a person's name, an inventive word". "The more inventive the company name is, the stronger protection
  it will have." https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet/nimiohjeet.html
- The YTJ guide says it is common to combine a place name with the line of business:
  https://www.ytj.fi/stc/ytjliitteet/y3opas.pdf (search snippet).
- Trade Names Act §8 (WIPO copy): a name that only states the type of business, generic goods/services or a commonly
  used place name is distinctive only once it is established. https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/463489
- Assessment (mine, not PRH's): "Hämeen Films" = place name + descriptive word. That is the combination PRH lists as
  acceptable, so outright rejection for lack of distinctiveness looks unlikely. But neither part is protectable on its
  own, and it may be refused if a similar "Häme… Film(s)" name is already registered in the same field. PRH will not
  confirm in advance (https://prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yritystennimet.html). Filing up to three alternatives
  lowers the risk.
