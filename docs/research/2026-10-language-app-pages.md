# Research: language-learning app landing pages (Oct 2026)

Pages studied: babbel.com, busuu.com, memrise.com, languagedrops.com, lingvist.com, clozemaster.com,
ankidroid.org, rosettastone.com, lingq.com, worddive.com, speakly.me, uusikielemme.fi (Duolingo via search
results; Mondly blocked).

## Patterns
- Hero = short promise + qualifier; free is top-billed when true (Duolingo, AnkiDroid).
- 3–4 benefit grid under the hero (icon + heading + one line), then alternating screenshot/feature rows.
- Concrete content counts are common and honest ("5,000+ words" Drops). FinnSana: "nearly 5,000 words",
  "over 100 grammar lessons".
- Spaced repetition named outright. CEFR labels are rare on homepages → "A1–B2" is a differentiator.
- Social proof (user counts, ratings, awards, press) — NOT available for FinnSana; honest substitutes:
  transparency facts (free, no ads, no account), content counts, maker note, testing status, feedback email.

## Google Play badge rules (partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com → Google Play → badges)
- Only "Get it on Google Play" and "Pre-register" badges exist. **There is no "Coming soon" badge**;
  use a plain text button until the listing is public. Never alter the badge; min height 28 px;
  clear space ¼ of height; legal line "Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC."

## Structured data (developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/software-app)
`MobileApplication` with name, description, operatingSystem "Android", applicationCategory
"EducationalApplication", offers price 0 EUR, publisher Organization Kuvadoo. **No aggregateRating** (no real
ratings; copying Play stars is against Google's rules). Star rich result not eligible yet — that's fine.

## Open Graph
og:title, og:type, og:image (1200×630, absolute URL), og:url, og:description, og:site_name, og:locale;
twitter:card summary_large_image.

## SEO themes
learn Finnish app · free Finnish app · Finnish vocabulary with pictures/audio · Finnish grammar cases ·
Finnish verb types · partitive · spoken Finnish (puhekieli) · CEFR A1–B2 Finnish · Finnish flashcards /
spaced repetition. Competitors are grammar references (uusikielemme.fi) or paid courses (WordDive,
Speakly) → room for "free, no ads, structured A1–B2 with grammar".

## Researcher suggestions we must NOT adopt (not in facts)
"No in-app purchases", "5x faster", "native speakers", YKI prep, "data stays on device".
