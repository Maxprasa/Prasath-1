Grammar

Finnish grammar that finally makes sense

Over 100 grammar lessons: cases, verb types, partitive, past tense, conditional, passive and more.

  • Spoken lesson explanations (computer-generated voice)
Spoken Finnish

Understand real spoken Finnish (puhekieli)

Finnish people speak differently from how they write. FinnSana explains standard Finnish (kirjakieli) and spoken Finnish (puhekieli) side by side.

  • 25 spoken Finnish lessons
  • “What did they say?”: hear a sentence in spoken Finnish and choose the standard sentence that means the same
  • Match spoken and standard pairs

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is FinnSana free?

Yes. FinnSana is free and has no ads.

How do I start?

Choose your level, from Beginner (A1) to Upper intermediate (B2), or start from the beginning. Start as a guest, and your progress stays on your phone. If you want a backup, continue with Google or with email, on the first screen or later in Me → Account & sync.

Can I try FinnSana now?

Yes, on Android. FinnSana is in testing on Google Play: ask for early access by email and tell us the email address you use on Google Play. We will then send you the link to the test version.

Which phones does it work on, and when is the release?

FinnSana is for Android. It is in testing on Google Play, and the public release is coming soon. It is also coming soon to the App Store.

Are the voices and pictures made by people?

No. Words, example sentences and lesson explanations are spoken by a clear computer-generated Finnish voice, at normal or slow speed. The pictures are AI-generated illustrations.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the sounds and pictures have downloaded. That is about 700 MB, once. Wi-Fi is best. The first lessons work right away.

Is FinnSana connected to any test or authority?

No. FinnSana is an independent app and is not connected to any authority or test organiser.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Me tab → tap your profile → Delete account. Without the app, see Delete your account.

How do I give feedback or report a mistake?

Write to kuvadoo@gmail.com.
