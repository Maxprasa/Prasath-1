FAQ

Questions and answers

Is FinnSana free?

Yes. FinnSana is free and has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use FinnSana without an account. If you like, you can sign in with Google or email to back up your progress.

Which levels does FinnSana cover?

CEFR levels A1 to B2: everyday words by topic and over 100 grammar lessons.

Which devices does it run on?

FinnSana is an Android app.

When can I download it?

FinnSana is in testing on Google Play. The public release is coming soon.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the sounds and pictures have downloaded. That is about 700 MB, once. Wi-Fi is best.

Are the voices real people?

No. Words, example sentences and lesson explanations are spoken by a clear computer-generated Finnish voice, at normal or slow speed. The pictures are AI-generated illustrations.

Does it teach spoken Finnish?

Yes. FinnSana explains spoken Finnish (puhekieli).

Is FinnSana connected to an official test or authority?

No. FinnSana is an independent app and is not connected to any authority or test organiser.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Me → Account → Delete account. Without the app, see Delete your account.

How do I give feedback or report a mistake?

Write to kuvadoo@gmail.com.
