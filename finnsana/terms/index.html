FinnSana terms of use

Last updated: 7 October 2026. The same terms are in the app under Me → Terms of use.

Who offers FinnSana

FinnSana is made by Kuvadoo, a sole trader business in Finland. Email: kuvadoo@gmail.com.

By using FinnSana you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, please do not use the app.

Using the app

FinnSana is for learning Finnish. You may use it for your own learning, as often as you like, on your own devices.

The lessons, exercises, pictures and recordings were made for FinnSana. Please do not copy, sell or publish them elsewhere. Content we use from others (see “Content from others”) you may reuse under its own licence.

Do not try to break, overload or misuse the app or its backup service, for example by creating accounts with a script.

Your account

An account is optional. Without one, your progress stays on your phone. With one (Google or email), your progress and the words you add yourself are backed up so you can get them back. The privacy policy explains what is stored and where.

Keep your password to yourself. You can delete your account at any time in Me → your profile → Delete account. We may close an account that is used to misuse the service.

The words and notes you add yourself stay yours. We store them only to back them up for you.

Computer-made content

All voices in FinnSana are computer-generated (Microsoft Azure AI Speech). The word pictures were made with AI image tools.

We work hard to keep the Finnish correct, but mistakes can still happen. If you find one, please tell us at kuvadoo@gmail.com and we will fix it.

FinnSana helps you learn, but we cannot promise that you reach a certain level or pass a language test or exam. FinnSana is an independent app and is not connected to any official test or authority.

If these terms change, the new version is shown in the app and on this page with a new date.
