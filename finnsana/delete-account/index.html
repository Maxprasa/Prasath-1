Delete your FinnSana account and data

FinnSana: Learn Finnish is made by Kuvadoo. You can use FinnSana without an account. If you signed in with Google or with an email address to back up your progress, you can delete that account and all of its cloud data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FinnSana.
  2. Go to the Me tab and tap your profile at the top (it opens Account & sync).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you signed in with email, enter your password to confirm.

Your FinnSana account and its cloud backup are deleted at once. The progress saved on your phone stays, and you can keep using FinnSana as a guest. Another phone that is still signed in to the deleted account cannot upload its progress to it again.

Without the app

Email kuvadoo@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my FinnSana account”. We delete the account and its backup within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted

  • Your FinnSana account (your name, email address, profile picture and account ID as stored by FinnSana).
  • Your cloud backup: lesson and review progress, streak, study time, settings, best scores and the words you added yourself.

What is kept

  • Nothing in your cloud backup is kept. So that the deleted backup cannot be re-created, a record holding only the random ID of the deleted account (no name, email or progress) is kept. Google keeps its security logs of sign-ins (IP address, device and app version) for a limited time, as described in the privacy policy.
  • Progress on your own phone is not touched. Me → Reset progress clears your learning progress, settings and best scores but keeps the words you added yourself (delete those in My words).
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone.
