Delete your FinnSana account and data

FinnSana: Learn Finnish is made by Kuvadoo. You can use FinnSana without an account. If you signed in, here is how to delete your account and data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open FinnSana.
  2. Go to Me → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.

What is deleted

This deletes your sign-in account and your cloud backup (progress, settings).

Progress on your phone

Your progress on the phone is removed with Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

Can't open the app?

Write to kuvadoo@gmail.com from the email address of your account and we delete it within 30 days.

Request deletion by email
